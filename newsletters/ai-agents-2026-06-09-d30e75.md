### AWS OpenSearch Serverless Gets a True Serverless Overhaul
AWS has delivered a significant architectural rebuild for OpenSearch Serverless, addressing a long-standing pain point for developers. Previously, the "serverless" offering still mandated a minimum of four OpenSearch Compute Units (OCUs), leading to constant billing even during idle periods. The core issue was a lack of full compute-storage decoupling, making it expensive for intermittent workloads. The new architecture, featuring 97% new code, achieves true serverless behavior by allowing compute to scale down to zero during inactivity (with a 10-minute idle timeout) and boasting 20x faster autoscaling.

**Key Developer-Friendly Insights:**
*   **Cost Optimization for Bursty Workloads:** For AI agents, RAG pipelines, and vector search, which often have bursty usage patterns with long idle stretches, this update translates to estimated 60% cost savings compared to provisioned clusters. Developers can now run their vector databases without fear of excessive billing for inactivity.
*   **Enhanced Scalability & Responsiveness:** The ability to spin up compute in seconds and achieve 20x faster autoscaling directly benefits agent architectures that need to respond quickly to spikes in demand without pre-provisioning costly resources.
*   **Simplified Infrastructure Management:** True serverless means less capacity planning and more focus on agent logic, as the underlying infrastructure intelligently handles scaling for varying loads.
